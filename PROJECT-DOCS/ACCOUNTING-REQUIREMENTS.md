# Accounting Requirements

## Core Principles
- Double-entry bookkeeping
- Every posting must balance
- Immutable transaction history
- Controlled reversals instead of silent deletion
- Full user/action audit trail

## Company Setup
- Legal name
- Registration details
- Tax identifiers
- Financial year
- Reporting currency
- Country
- Accounting framework

## Chart of Accounts
- Assets
- Liabilities
- Equity
- Revenue
- Cost of sales
- Expenses
- Other income/expense

## Transactions
- Journals
- Sales invoices
- Purchase bills
- Receipts
- Payments
- Credit notes
- Debit notes
- Accruals
- Prepayments
- Adjustments

## Subledgers
- Accounts receivable
- Accounts payable
- Bank
- Cash
- Fixed assets
- Inventory
- Payroll integration later

## Reports
- General ledger
- Trial balance
- Profit and Loss
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow
- Changes in Equity
- AR ageing
- AP ageing
- Bank reconciliation
- Account activity
- Comparative reporting

## Controls
- Maker/checker approval
- Period locks
- User permissions
- Posting limits
- Journal approval
- Reversal control
- Closing process

## IFRS Direction
The system should support IFRS-oriented accounting workflows.

Do not copy copyrighted IFRS standard text directly into the product without confirming licensing rights.

Store:
- standard reference
- policy
- workflow
- checklist
- compliance result
- evidence

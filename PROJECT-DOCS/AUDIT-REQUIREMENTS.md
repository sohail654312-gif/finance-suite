# Audit Requirements

## Standards Direction
Audit workflows should be designed around International Standards on Auditing principles.

Professional judgement remains with the auditor.
Software assists; it does not replace the auditor.

## Engagement Lifecycle

### Acceptance
- Client acceptance
- Continuance
- Independence
- Conflict checks
- Engagement letter

### Planning
- Understanding the entity
- Industry
- Materiality
- Performance materiality
- Audit strategy
- Team assignment

### Risk Assessment
- Financial statement risks
- Assertion-level risks
- Significant risks
- Fraud risks
- Internal controls

## Assertions
- Existence
- Completeness
- Accuracy
- Valuation
- Rights and obligations
- Occurrence
- Cut-off
- Classification
- Presentation

## Audit Procedures
- Inspection
- Observation
- Confirmation
- Recalculation
- Reperformance
- Analytical procedures
- Inquiry

## Working Papers
Each working paper should record:
- Reference
- Client
- Period
- Audit area
- Objective
- Procedure
- Sample
- Evidence
- Exceptions
- Conclusion
- Prepared by
- Reviewed by
- Dates

## Evidence
Support attachments such as:
- invoices
- contracts
- bank statements
- confirmations
- payroll
- tax returns
- board minutes
- inventory records

## Audit Completion
- Misstatement summary
- Going concern
- Subsequent events
- Related parties
- Management representation
- Partner review
- Audit opinion

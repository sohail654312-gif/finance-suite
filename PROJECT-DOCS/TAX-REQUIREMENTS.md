# Tax Requirements

## Design Principle
There is no single global tax system.

Finance Suite will use:

GLOBAL TAX ENGINE
+
COUNTRY TAX PACKS

## Global Tax Engine
- Tax periods
- Tax codes
- Tax rates
- Tax adjustments
- Tax reconciliation
- Tax provisions
- Deferred tax
- Related-party transactions
- Supporting documents

## Country Packs
Possible future packs:

### United Kingdom
- VAT
- Corporation Tax
- PAYE-related integration
- Making Tax Digital considerations

### Pakistan
- Income tax
- Sales tax
- Withholding taxes
- FBR-related workflows

### Saudi Arabia
- VAT
- Zakat / Corporate Income Tax support
- Withholding tax
- ZATCA-oriented workflows

### UAE
- VAT
- Corporate Tax

## Tax Computation
Accounting Profit
+ Non-deductible expenses
+ Tax adjustments
- Allowances
- Reliefs
= Taxable Profit

## Controls
- Tax return status
- Reviewer
- Filing deadline
- Payment deadline
- Supporting evidence
- Tax authority reference
- Period locking

## Important
Tax logic must be versioned by:
- Country
- Tax year
- Effective date
- Rule version
